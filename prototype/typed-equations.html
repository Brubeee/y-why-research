This follow-up checks one fixed example only. A repeated incorrect result is a recurrence signal, not independent verification of misconception or learning. No learner-trait claim is made; a teacher should review the full work and context.

What this can tell you

The checker compares equations as mathematical solution sets. A rule-based note may describe a change, such as adding a constant to only one side. These notes are tentative patterns in the written steps; they do not establish what a learner was thinking.

Unsupported notation, ambiguous lines, or cases the small rule set cannot explain are marked for teacher review. There is no trained model behind this screen and no teacher validation of its explanations yet.
